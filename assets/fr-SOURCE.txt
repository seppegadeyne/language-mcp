Cross-check of the download: upstream publishes no checksum. The "classique"
files in the same archive (fr-classique.dic b78a868e...c88c, fr-classique.aff
c176610c...2de5) are byte-identical to fr_FR/dictionaries/fr.dic and fr.aff in
https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries at commit
8a58c570f0e9cb1d908db15c878c1f371a793d8b, an independent distribution channel
of the same Dicollecte release. LibreOffice ships the "classique" variant; this
repository bundles "toutes variantes" from the official archive instead.

Why "toutes variantes": website copy can follow either spelling system, and a
spell check must not flag a valid reform spelling or a valid traditional one.
Choose the stricter "classique" or "reforme1990" files only if a project fixes
one system; replace fr.dic and fr.aff together.
