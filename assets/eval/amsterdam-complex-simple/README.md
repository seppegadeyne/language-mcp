# Amsterdam complex–simple sentence pairs (evaluation corpus)

1311 automatically aligned sentence pairs (complex, simplified) from ~50
municipal documents rewritten by communications experts of the City of
Amsterdam, published as the "Dataset by City of Amsterdam" in
https://github.com/Amsterdam-AI-Team/dutch-municipal-text-simplification

runtime by any tool.
